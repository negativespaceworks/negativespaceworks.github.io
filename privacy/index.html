Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Negative Space Works (“we”) makes Mac software, including Kompo and Driftile. This policy describes how we handle information. If our practices change, we will update this page.

Our apps

Kompo and Driftile are designed to work on your Mac. They do not require an account, and they do not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

  • Kompo reads audio from Music.app or Spotify on your Mac so it can draw a spectrum analyzer. That audio is processed on your device. Preferences stay on your Mac.
  • Driftile is a screen saver. Preferences stay on your Mac.

We do not receive audio, screen contents, or app preferences from these products over the network.

This website

This site is a static website. We do not run accounts or forms on it.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see aggregate traffic and performance, including page views, referrers, countries, browsers, and Core Web Vitals. It does not use cookies, does not fingerprint browsers, and does not track people across other sites. Cloudflare discards IP addresses at the edge and does not store them with this analytics data.

The host (GitHub Pages) and Cloudflare (DNS and CDN) may also keep ordinary request logs such as IP address and the pages requested.

Email

If you write to support@negativespace.works, we receive whatever you send (for example your address, message, and any attachments) so we can reply. We use that mail for support, not for advertising.

Purchases

If you buy software through the Mac App Store, Apple handles payment and account information under Apple’s privacy policy. We do not receive your card details.

Your requests

To ask what we hold about you, or to ask us to delete support mail we have from you, email support@negativespace.works.
