About

Negative Space Works is a personal studio that makes small works for time at the computer.

The first ideas arrived during remote days. Long hours at a screen. Quiet rooms. Small gaps between tasks where something unnecessary but welcome could live. Remote work was the starting point. The work is not limited to it. Anyone who spends time at a computer knows those gaps.

We make software that adds a quiet point of color to that time. Not a loud product. Not another dashboard. A small presence beside the work—something you can leave on, glance at, or return to when the day opens a little.

“Color” here is not bright branding. The mark and the site stay mostly monochrome on purpose. Color belongs in the works: a calm mark in negative space—a spectrum that moves with music, a field that drifts when the screen rests, a softer ground around a call, a mirrored slice you choose to share. Things that color the hour without asking for the whole hour.

The works stay intentionally small. Small enough to finish with care. Small enough to stay honest about what they do. Quality and attention stay high because the surface stays narrow.

The works came from that same place. Kompo turns music from Music.app or Spotify into a vintage-style spectrum analyzer. Driftile lets color fields drift across a resting screen. Reliefo softens everything around a meeting so the call can hold the front. Mirrame mirrors a chosen rectangle into a small always-on-top window for selective sharing. Different jobs. Same scale. Same quiet.

That is the studio: small works for the quiet spaces in digital work.
