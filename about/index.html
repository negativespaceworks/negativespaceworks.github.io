About

Negative space is what lies just beyond what takes center stage.

We give shape to the small possibilities we find there.

Negative Space Works is an independent studio making small works for time spent at the computer.

It began with a small idea about remote work.

Spending more time working through a screen made us notice things we had rarely thought about before. Not the work itself, but the little things around it. The moments before a call. What stays on the screen while sharing it. The small interruptions between one task and the next.

None of them were big problems. Most were hardly problems at all.

But there was something interesting in those spaces. Small things that could work a little better. Feel a little calmer. Or simply become a little more enjoyable.

We started thinking of them as negative spaces.

Not empty spaces, and not things that do not matter. Just the spaces outside whatever happens to be at the center of our attention.

Once we started looking, we found them everywhere.

While working. While listening to music. While looking at a screen, and while looking away from it. In the moments between doing one thing and the next.

That way of looking at things is still at the center of what we do.

Sometimes what we find becomes a useful tool. Sometimes an experiment. Sometimes just a small thing that makes time at the computer a little nicer.

We do not know where the next one will be.

But we like looking just outside the center.

These are small works for the quiet spaces in digital work.
